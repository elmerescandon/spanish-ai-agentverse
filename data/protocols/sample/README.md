# Synthetic sample protocol: SYN-T2D-301 (GLYDE-2)

`SYN-T2D-301_protocol.pdf` is a fictional 15-page Phase 3 type 2 diabetes
protocol (oral GLP-1 agonist added to metformin, versus placebo). It follows
the layout of the real demo protocols in `../demo/`: title page,
compliance statement, contents, synopsis, Schedule of Activities, objectives
and endpoints table, design, eligibility, intervention, assessments,
statistics and ethics. Upload it on `/demo` like any other protocol.

It is generated by `scripts/make_sample_protocol.py` (`make sample-protocol`).
Edit the script, not the PDF. `tests/test_sample_protocol.py` fails if the
two drift apart.

## Planted issues (answer key)

Each issue is deliberate, so an agent run can be scored against this list.

| # | Agent | Issue | Where (page) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Completeness | Primary endpoint is HbA1c at Week 26, but the Schedule of Activities has no HbA1c at V5 (Week 26) | 7 vs 5 |
| 2 | Completeness | Sample size gives power, alpha, SD and effect, but no dropout rate | 13 |
| 3 | Completeness | No independent Data Monitoring Committee and no stopping rules for a 600-person Phase 3; only the sponsor's medical monitor | 14 |
| 4 | Completeness | Estimand only names the comparison; intercurrent events (rescue medication, stopping drug) are not defined under ICH E9(R1) | 7 |
| 5 | Completeness | Consent says "12 clinic visits", the Schedule of Activities has 8 (V1 to V8) | 14 vs 5 |
| 6 | Completeness | NCT number is "Pending"; no registry ID | 1 |
| 7 | Peer comparison | eGFR >= 60 is stricter than peers (usually >= 30 or >= 45) | 9 |
| 8 | Peer comparison | HbA1c window 7.5% to 9.5% is narrower than peers (about 7.0% to 10.5%) | 9 |
| 9 | Peer comparison / cohort | Upper age limit of 70 removes older adults, who make up much of the T2DM population | 9 |
| 10 | Peer comparison / cohort | Any prior insulin or GLP-1 use excluded, and a 12-week washout for other agents | 9 |
| 11 | Cohort | "Able to read and speak English" removes non-English speakers in a 6-country trial | 9 |
| 12 | Cohort | Cardiovascular event within 12 months and NYHA class II to IV heart failure excluded | 9 |

Values the parser should extract: Phase 3, randomized, parallel, quadruple
masked (participant, investigator, outcomes assessor, sponsor), 3 arms,
target enrollment 600, ages 18 to 70 inclusive, about 85 sites in 6
countries, 90% power, alpha 0.05, 10 inclusion and 15 exclusion criteria.
